5 — Assembly Order

  1. 1Cut side rails to 170 cm from the long bed-frame boards
  2. 2Attach end rails at both ends with 2 screws each → outer rectangle formed
  3. 3Screw 4 legs (15 cm) flush to the four corners underneath
  4. 4Mark hinge point at 110 cm from foot end on both side rails
  5. 5Lay and screw 8 slats on fixed section, 6 slats on backrest section
  6. 6Bolt backrest to fixed section at hinge with 2 bolts (reuse barn-door hinges)
  7. 7Cut 3 notches in the fixed-side end of the side rails for the prop (~100°, 120°, 140°)
  8. 8Sand all surfaces, apply wood oil for outdoor use
